Is cancelling as easy as signing up?

cancel-parity runs your sign-up and cancel flows in Playwright, counts the screens and clicks each one takes, flags retention tricks on every screen, and fails the build when cancelling is harder.

StreamFlix is a mock streaming site with one sign-up flow and two ways to cancel. CI runs the sign-up flow against each cancel flow on every build of main and publishes both reports here.
